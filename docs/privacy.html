ABOUT THIS WEBSITE

Website privacy

Updated October 31, 2026

This notice describes the public Hearth product website. The Hearth app is in development; its app privacy policy will be published separately before public release.

A simple place to read about Hearth

This website has no sign-in, contact form, checkout, advertising, or analytics scripts. Its code does not set cookies or save information in your browser. Fonts use your device’s installed typefaces, and illustrations and styles are served with the site.

Hosting

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitor IP addresses for security when serving Pages websites. You can read more in GitHub’s explanation of Pages and its General Privacy Statement.

External links

If you follow a link to another website, that service handles your visit according to its own privacy information.
