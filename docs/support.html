Frequently asked questions

What does Hearth do?

Hearth is an app in development for keeping appliance and home-system details, maintenance plans, and service records together. It brings available maintenance sources, replacement estimates, and recall notices alongside the information you record.

Can I download Hearth yet?

Hearth is being developed for iPhone and Android. Public App Store and Google Play downloads are not yet available, and a release date has not been announced.

Can I sign up on this website?

This website introduces Hearth. It does not create app accounts or offer a testing signup. If you already have a testing invitation, follow the access instructions included with it.

Where does the maintenance guidance come from?

Available catalog guidance draws on manufacturer information and public sources for supported equipment. Source references are shown where supplied. Maintenance plans also use the dates and history you record; your own equipment’s manual remains the reference for its care.

Are replacement estimates and recall notices complete?

Coverage varies by model and source. Replacement estimates are planning aids, not predictions or proof of part compatibility. An absent recall match does not establish that an appliance is safe. Check official notices and your manufacturer’s instructions.

Are the home records shown here real?

The illustrated house and maintenance plan on this website use fictional records to show the idea behind Hearth.

Help for existing testers

Use the contact channel in your testing invitation.

That is the best place to ask about access, report an issue, or get help with your test account and data.

When reporting an issue, include your device type, the app version if available, and what happened. Please leave out passwords, sign-in codes, payment details, and private home information that is not needed to explain the issue.
