ABOUT HEARTH

Care for the place you call home.

Hearth is an app in development for organizing appliances, home systems, maintenance plans, and service records. Its purpose is to make the details of caring for a home easier to find and keep.

One place for the useful details

Model numbers, installation dates, photos, and service notes are easy to lose track of. Hearth brings them together so you can see what’s at home, keep a record of completed care, and find the details when you need them again.

Add the information you know. Leave unknown dates open. Build a useful history over time, one appliance and one maintenance visit at a time.

Guidance with its sources in view

Alongside your records, Hearth brings together available maintenance sources, replacement estimates, and recall notices for supported equipment. Category guidance is kept distinct from information verified for a particular model.

Coverage varies. An estimated interval does not predict when your appliance will fail, and a category template does not confirm an exact part fit. Follow your equipment’s manual and official recall notices, and confirm compatibility before buying parts.

A record to return to

Hearth is designed to support everyday record keeping and reminders. It does not replace a qualified professional’s inspection, repairs, or safety advice.

Still taking shape

Hearth is in development for iPhone and Android. App Store and Google Play downloads are not yet available, and a public release date has not been announced.
