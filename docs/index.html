FROM “I SHOULD” TO “IT’S HERE”

Less to keep in your head.

Build a useful record over time, one item and one maintenance visit at a time.

01 / GATHER

Know what’s at home.

Keep appliance models, installation dates, photos, and home systems together. Add the details you know and leave unknown dates open.

02 / PLAN

See what needs care.

Review maintenance tasks and available guidance based on supported equipment and the history you record. Check the source and your own manual.

03 / REMEMBER

Keep the story.

Record completed maintenance, service notes, and who helped. Return to the details when it’s time for the next visit or replacement.

USEFUL CONTEXT, CLEAR LIMITS

A helpful reference
for your next step.

Hearth brings available maintenance sources, replacement estimates, and recall notices alongside your records. Category guidance is labeled separately from verified model information.

Coverage is incomplete. Estimates do not establish part compatibility, predict a failure, or confirm that an appliance is safe. Follow your manufacturer’s instructions and official recall notices.
